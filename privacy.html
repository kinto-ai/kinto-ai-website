Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Who is responsible

The controller of the personal data collected through this site is Kendal Segura (KINTO AI), based in Turrialba, Cartago, Costa Rica. Contact: kendal@kintoai.com.

What data is collected and why

This site has no forms of its own and no analytics tools. Personal data is collected only when you book a call:

  • Name and email address, along with any information you choose to write when booking. They are used exclusively to coordinate the call, follow up on what was discussed and answer your inquiry.

Your data is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

Call recording

Booked calls take place over video conference and are recorded and transcribed using the Fathom tool, in order to follow up faithfully on what was discussed. You are informed of this practice when booking; at the start of the call you may ask not to be recorded and the meeting continues without recording.

Third-party services this site uses

  • Cal.com: processes call bookings (name, email and chosen time slot) under its own privacy policies.
  • Fathom: records and transcribes video calls, under its own policies.
  • Google Fonts and the Cloudflare CDN (cdnjs): the site loads fonts and icons from these services; when it does, your browser transmits your IP address to those providers.
  • Vercel: hosts this site and may log technical access data (such as IP addresses) in its server logs.

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics or advertising tools. The third-party services mentioned may use their own technical cookies when loading their components (for example, the Cal.com calendar).

Your rights

Under Costa Rica's Law No. 8968 on the Protection of Individuals regarding the Processing of their Personal Data, you may at any time request access to, correction or deletion of your data, and withdraw your consent. To exercise these rights write to kendal@kintoai.com; your request will be handled within a reasonable time.

Retention

Contact data and recordings are kept while there is an active business relationship or an ongoing conversation, and are deleted when no longer needed or upon your request.

Changes to this policy

Any change to this policy will be published on this page, with its update date.
